When you travel with Naaz, you travel with family. JazakAllah Khair for trusting us.

What happens if the Saudi Riyal rate changes?
Our packages are quoted in INR. However, if there is a massive fluctuation (>5%) in the SAR/INR rate before your final payment, a nominal difference may apply as per our terms and conditions.
Are your package prices inclusive of GST?
Yes, all our listed package prices are inclusive of 18% GST. There are no hidden charges.
Do you provide training for first-time pilgrims?
Yes! We conduct exclusive pre-departure orientation sessions in Vijayapura where we teach you the complete Manasik (rituals), Duas, and practical tips.
